How it works

  1. Enter what recursTap + and add a name, price, currency, billing rhythm (every N days, weeks, months or years), first payment date, category, card and how many people share it. Turn on Free trial and set its end date when a trial starts first.
  2. Read the Line and payday splitThe Line tab orders upcoming charges by day with a running total. Set your payday under Settings > Payday and the line splits at every payday so you can see what leaves before the next cheque.
  3. Handle announced price rises and one-off chargesInside any subscription, tap A price change is coming to schedule a future rate from a specific date without changing earlier charges. Long-press a charge on the Line or tap the menu on a charge row to skip it or record a one-time amount.
  4. Review, pause, cancel and savePause a service with Put on hold to stop upcoming charges and alerts until Back on. Mark cancelled services as ended to tally Saved by cancelling on the Year tab, or run Start the check-up to review active plans from highest yearly cost downward.

Questions & Answers

Does Subscriptions cancel services for me?

No. Subscriptions does not link to providers or terminate accounts on your behalf. Cancel directly with the service, then tap Mark as cancelled in the app and record a cancellation link inside Notes if helpful.

Does it connect to my bank or scan my emails?

No. Subscriptions reads nothing from bank accounts, cards, inboxes or the App Store. Every entry stays local and reflects only what you type in.

How are multiple currencies counted?

Each currency keeps its own separate total across the Line and the Year tab. The app does not perform automatic currency conversion.

How do totals and widgets update?

Tapping How it is counted in Settings explains real charge dates, monthly/yearly averages, month-end clamping, holds and split shares. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets refresh automatically at midnight and whenever you edit your list.

What is included free, and what unlocks with Pro?

Up to 5 subscriptions, the Line, payday splits, price changes, single-charge edits, Heads-up alerts, Check-up, savings tracking, widgets, JSON backup files and Siri questions are free. Subscriptions Pro is a one-time in-app purchase for unlimited subscriptions, local charge/trial reminders, the 12-month Year chart with category/card breakdowns and CSV export.

How do I move my list or get a refund?

Use Settings > Save a backup file and Restore from a backup on a new iPhone. Use Settings > Restore Purchase if you switched devices after buying Pro. Refunds for App Store purchases are processed by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
