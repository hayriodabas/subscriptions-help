On-device storage and widget

Subscriptions writes your list to a local file inside your iPhone app storage and a local shared container so your Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets can show upcoming charges without network access. Preferences such as reminder hour, payday schedule and currency filters stay in local UserDefaults.

Notifications and Siri

When you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device using iOS UserNotifications based on the dates in your list. Siri Shortcuts answer questions about next renewals and monthly totals locally using on-device data.

In-app purchase

Subscriptions Pro is offered as a single non-consumable in-app purchase processed exclusively by Apple through StoreKit 2. We never receive your payment method, billing address or Apple ID.

Backups and CSV export

When you tap Save a backup file or Export as CSV, iOS lets you save or share that file directly via Files, AirDrop or Mail. Neither file leaves your device unless you choose where to send it.
